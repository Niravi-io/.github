<h1 align="center">Niravi</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Video intelligence platform</b> — search, recall, and analysis over your video corpus.</p>

Niravi turns raw video into a queryable asset: moment-level search, transcripts, scenes, speakers, and AI-assisted recall over everything you ingest — through the web app, a public API, SDKs (Python & TypeScript), and an MCP server.
