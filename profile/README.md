<h1 align="center">Niravi</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Video Intelligence.</b> We turn unstructured video into structured, queryable data.</p>

Niravi builds models that understand video, from a creator’s footage to the cameras and lidar on cars, drones and robots. Every moment becomes time-coded data that people, apps and AI agents can search.

- **Video Transformation Model**: a video in, structured, time-coded data out. Shots and scenes, the transcript with speakers, people, on-screen text, sound, a summary and search vectors. Shipped.
- **Perception Model**: built on the transformation model, tuned for the cameras, lidar and logs that cars, drones and robots carry. In pilots.

Two applications run on them:

- **[Niravi ACE](https://niravi.io/ace/)**: a Mac app for creators. Find any moment, edit by the words, caption and publish. Free to start.
- **[Niravi Arrow API](https://niravi.io/arrow/)**: the models in your systems, over REST, a TypeScript SDK and an MCP server.
